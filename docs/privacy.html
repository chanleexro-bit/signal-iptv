Signal IPTV

Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026 · Applies to the Signal IPTV app for Android

Signal IPTV is a video player for M3U playlists. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: the app doesn’t collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal information.

Information we collect

None. Signal IPTV has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting services. The developer does not receive any information about you or how you use the app.

Information stored on your device

To remember your settings, the app saves the following only on your phone:

  • the playlist you last loaded and any playlist link you typed in
  • your favorite channels and recently watched channels
  • the TV guide sources you chose

This information never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Connections to other servers

To show channels and the TV guide, the app connects directly from your phone to the servers that host the playlists, guides and video streams you choose (for example the public iptv-org lists, or a link you enter yourself). Like any website, those servers can see your IP address and basic device information when your phone connects to them. Those servers are run by third parties, not by Signal IPTV, and their own privacy policies apply.

Files you open

If you open a playlist file from your phone, the app reads it only to show its channels. The file is not uploaded anywhere.

Children

Signal IPTV is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project’s page: github.com/chanleexro-bit/signal-iptv/issues.

Signal IPTV doesn’t host, provide or sell any TV channels or video content.
